Learning objectives

At the end of this section, you should be able to:

  • Analyze the analytical properties of a function, including its domain, intercepts, critical points, intervals of monotonicity, concavity, and points of inflection.
  • Apply first- and second-derivative tests to determine local and global extrema and the concavity of the function.
  • Construct an accurate qualitative sketch of the graph of a function based on its analytical characteristics.

1Before sketchingEven and odd functions
Even functionsymmetric about the $y$-axis
$f(-x)=f(x)$ for every $x$ in $D_f$.   Example: $f(x)=x^2$
$y=x^2$: mirror image in the $y$-axis
Odd functionsymmetric about the origin
$f(-x)=-f(x)$ for every $x$ in $D_f$.   Example: $f(x)=x^3$
$y=x^3$: unchanged by a half-turn about the origin
تذكير سريع بالدالة الزوجية والفردية والتماثل. عرض فقط بدون أسئلة.
1Before sketchingIntercepts with the axes
Intercepts
  • $y$-intercept: put $x=0$ and compute $y=f(0)$.
  • $x$-intercepts: put $y=0$ and solve $f(x)=0$.
Example
Find the intercepts of $f(x)=x^2-4$.
$y$-intercept: $x=0 \Rightarrow y=0-4=-4$
$\Rightarrow$ the point $(0,-4)$
$x$-intercepts: $y=0 \Rightarrow x^2-4=0$
$(x-2)(x+2)=0 \Rightarrow x=\pm2$
$\Rightarrow$ the points $(2,0)$ and $(-2,0)$
$f(x)=x^2-4$
مع محور y نضع x = 0، ومع محور x نضع y = 0. المثال فرق بين مربعين.

1Before sketchingSlant asymptote
Slant asymptote(oblique asymptote)
A slant asymptote is a straight line $y=mx+b$ such that $$\lim_{x\to\pm\infty}\left[f(x)-(mx+b)\right]=0.$$The vertical distance between the graph of $f$ and the line approaches zero as $x\to\pm\infty$.
For rational functions
When the degree of the numerator is exactly one greater than the degree of the denominator, long division gives $f(x)=mx+b+\dfrac{R(x)}{D(x)}$. As $x\to\pm\infty$, $\dfrac{R(x)}{D(x)}\to0$, so $y=mx+b$ is the slant asymptote.
Figure 4.4.1: the graph approaches the line $y=mx+b$

1Before sketchingExample 1
Example 1
Find the slant asymptote for the function $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2}{x-1}$.
Degree of numerator $=2$ $=$ degree of denominator $+1$
$f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-1+1}{x-1}=\dfrac{x^2-1}{x-1}+\dfrac{1}{x-1}$
$\phantom{f(x)}=x+1+\dfrac{1}{x-1}$
As $x\to\pm\infty$: $\dfrac{1}{x-1}\to0$
The slant asymptote is the line $y=x+1$
Figure 4.4.2: $y=\dfrac{x^2}{x-1}$ and its asymptotes

4Challenging & CommunicateChallenging 2 · constants
Challenging 2
Find constants $a,\ b$ and $c$ such that $f(x)=ax^3+bx^2+c$ will have a local extremum at $(2,11)$ and a point of inflection at $(1,5)$. Sketch the graph of $y=f(x)$.
$f'(x)=3ax^2+2bx$
$f''(x)=6ax+2b$
Local extremum at $x=2$: $f'(2)=0$
$12a+4b=0 \Rightarrow b=-3a$
Inflection at $x=1$: $f''(1)=0$
$6a+2b=0$ (same condition)
$f(1)=5$: $a+b+c=5 \Rightarrow c=5+2a$
$f(2)=11$: $8a+4b+c=11$
$8a-12a+5+2a=11 \Rightarrow -2a=6$
$\therefore\ a=-3,\quad b=9,\quad c=-1$
$f(x)=-3x^3+9x^2-1$
local min $(0,-1)$, local max $(2,11)$, inflection point $(1,5)$
(from $f'=-9x(x-2)$ and $f''=-18(x-1)$)
الشرطان على المشتقتين يعطيان نفس المعادلة b = −3a، فنحتاج شرطي القيمتين. الناتج −3x³ + 9x² − 1.

4Challenging & CommunicateCommunicate 4 · one straight line
Communicate 4
Show that the curve $f(x)=\dfrac{x+1}{x^2+1}$ has three points of inflection and they all lie on one straight line.
$f(1)=1 \Rightarrow$ $A(1,1)$
$f(-2+\sqrt3)=\dfrac{1+\sqrt3}{4} \Rightarrow$ $B$
$f(-2-\sqrt3)=\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{4} \Rightarrow$ $C$
slope of $AB=\dfrac{1-\frac{1+\sqrt3}{4}}{1-(-2+\sqrt3)}=\dfrac{\frac{3-\sqrt3}{4}}{3-\sqrt3}=\dfrac14$
slope of $AC=\dfrac{1-\frac{1-\sqrt3}{4}}{1-(-2-\sqrt3)}=\dfrac{\frac{3+\sqrt3}{4}}{3+\sqrt3}=\dfrac14$
$\therefore$ slope $AB=$ slope $AC$ with common point $A$
$\therefore\ A,\ B,\ C$ lie on one straight line  ■
the three inflection points on $y=\dfrac{x+3}{4}$
